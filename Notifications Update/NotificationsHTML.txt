<b><font color="green"> Engineering Guidelines.</font> SolarWinds will poll most objects:<br>
	
----> every 2 mins for response times and status (Up/Down)<br>
----> every 10 mins for statistics (Details, CPU, Memory, Utilization, Errors etc)<br><br>

SolarWinds Log Collectors will capture and process events from objects that have been configured for Syslog & Trap reporting.<br>

----> Response times to the events above are dependent on Alert thresholds - and some operational delays - which are applied in order 
to capture suboptimal operations that may indicate an issue or problem that needs investigating by the responsible team.<a href="https://natwest.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/DNT/pages/1057237106/Alerting" target="_blank"> Alert Details</a>
